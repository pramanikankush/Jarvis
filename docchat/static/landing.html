Capabilities

Upload PDFs, DOCX, TXT and MD. Jarvis chunks, embeds, and answers with hybrid search plus citations — source name, page, and passage.

Spreadsheet analysis

Ask about your CSV or XLSX in plain language — aggregations, filters, trends, outliers, and charts — without writing a line of code.

Tools & math

How it works

One agent. The right tool, automatically.

Every question flows through a single router — documents, data, web, memory, or the model itself. You see the high-level steps, never the reasoning.

01

Understand

Jarvis reads your question and figures out intent — conversational, document, data, or current-events.

02

Route

An intent router picks the tool: RAG, spreadsheet agent, web search, calculator, memory, or plain LLM.

03

Retrieve

Hybrid search blends vector similarity and keyword BM25, then reranks for the most relevant chunks.

04

Verify

Self-RAG checks that sources are relevant and the answer is actually supported by evidence.

05

Answer

Citations and sources come back with every answer — never fabricated, always distinguishable.
